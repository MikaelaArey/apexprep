Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version: ApexPrep has no ads, no tracking and no selling of your data. Your study progress is saved on your iPhone and backed up to our server so you don't lose it. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

What we store

  • Your study activity: the questions you answer, the choice you picked, whether it was right, how long you took and how sure you said you were. It also includes your review cards, XP, streaks, badges and settings, and any practice-test scores you log.
  • Your account: a random account ID, created automatically so your progress can back up without you signing in.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple: an identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one. We don't ask Apple for your name.

We don't collect your contacts, location, photos, health data or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To back up your progress and restore it if you sign in on another iPhone.
  • To show you your own stats, streaks and review schedule.
  • To show "how everyone answered" totals for a question. These are anonymous percentages, shown only after at least 30 students have answered, and never include who answered what.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored on your iPhone and backed up with Supabase, the database service we use. It travels over encrypted connections, and each account can read only its own data.

Things that stay on your phone

Study reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; we don't send push notifications from a server. The "Listen" feature uses your iPhone's built-in speech, so the text isn't sent to us.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers, data brokers or analytics companies. The only outside service that handles it is Supabase, which stores it on our behalf. We would disclose information only if the law required it.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your account and everything backed up with it, right away. Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. Backups from guest accounts that go unused for 90 days may also be deleted.

Children

ApexPrep is made for students preparing for the MCAT and LSAT and isn't directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
