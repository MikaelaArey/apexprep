Support

Questions, problems or a question you think is wrong? Email areybusiness23@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

I think a question has a mistake

Please email us the first few words of the question and what looks wrong. Every report gets checked, and confirmed errors are fixed in the next update.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?

Go to Profile → Account → Continue with Apple. On your new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID and your progress comes back. Until you sign in, your progress backs up as a guest, which only that iPhone can restore.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account → Delete account and data. Your account and everything backed up with it are deleted permanently, right away.

Are these official AAMC or LSAC questions?

No. They're original practice questions written to match each exam's format and topics. We recommend taking the official practice exams too, and you can log those scores in Profile → Score history.

Can I turn off Sprout and the game features?

Yes. In Profile, switch off Sprout, your study buddy, or turn on Pure Study to hide XP, levels, badges, Sprout and celebrations.

How do I change or stop reminders?

Go to Profile → Daily reminder. Reminders also back off on their own if you stop opening the app.
