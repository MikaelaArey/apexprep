2. Purpose and audience
ApexPrep is a free study app for students preparing for the MCAT (the medical school admission test) and the LSAT (the law school admission test), mostly college students and recent graduates. Students often prepare for months alongside classes and jobs, and find it hard to study regularly and learn from their mistakes. ApexPrep fits practice into short breaks:
- A "Daily 5" set of questions takes about three minutes.
- Every answer choice comes with an explanation of why it is right or wrong.
- Missed questions come back as review, scheduled around the student's test date.
- Stats show where to focus.

3. How to use it
No login, demo account or sample files are needed.
- On first launch, choose MCAT or LSAT, and optionally a test date and daily goal.
- Home: tap Start Daily 5, choose an answer, tap Check to see the explanation, then tap Next.
- Practice tab: Quick Drill, The Climb (questions from easiest to hardest), the ranked question bank and reading passages.
- Review tab: review of missed concepts.
- Stats tab: progress by section and difficulty.
- Profile (person icon, top right): score history, settings and Account.
- Accounts: the app creates an anonymous guest account automatically so progress is backed up. Profile → Account → Continue with Apple turns it into a Sign in with Apple account. This is optional.
- Account deletion: Profile → Account → Delete account and data permanently deletes the account and all of its data.

4. External services
- Supabase: the backend database and authentication service. It stores each student's backed-up progress and manages guest accounts and Sign in with Apple sessions.
- Sign in with Apple: optional sign-in.
- Vercel: hosts the support and privacy policy website.

The app uses no payment processors, AI services, analytics or advertising.

5. Regions
The app works the same in every region where it is available. The content is in English, and features and content don't differ by region. It is not offered in mainland China.
