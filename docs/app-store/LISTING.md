## Description

ApexPrep turns spare minutes into MCAT and LSAT progress. Every question explains why the right answer works and what trap each wrong answer sets, so you learn from every miss.

STUDY IN TWO-MINUTE GAPS
• Daily 5: five questions that mix reviews, your weakest topic and something new
• Quick Drill: pick a section, question type, difficulty level and length
• Passages with data tables, sized for your phone

LEARN FROM EVERY ANSWER
• A one-line summary, step-by-step reasoning and a takeaway for every question
• Every wrong choice names its trap, such as reversal, look-alike or misread data
• Missed and unsure questions come back as spaced review, scheduled around your test date

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
• Every question ranked within its section from Warm-up to Boss
• Stats by section, question type and difficulty, plus your most common mistakes
• Score history: log your practice tests and official scores and track them against your goal

STAY MOTIVATED
• The Climb: work through each section from easiest to hardest, ten questions a floor
• 26 badges, streaks with rest days, and Sprout, a study buddy who cheers you on
• Prefer it plain? Pure Study hides the game features and keeps everything else

WHAT'S INSIDE
• MCAT: 1,037 questions across Chem/Phys, CARS, Bio/Biochem and Psych/Soc, including 40 passages
• LSAT: 256 Logical Reasoning and Reading Comprehension questions

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. ApexPrep works offline, and your progress backs up automatically. Sign in with Apple to keep it when you switch phones, and delete your account from the app at any time.

ApexPrep's questions are original practice items, not official test content. MCAT® is a registered trademark of the Association of American Medical Colleges, and LSAT® is a registered trademark of the Law School Admission Council. Neither is affiliated with or endorses ApexPrep.

## App Review notes

> ApexPrep works without an account. Students study as guests, and their progress backs up automatically to an anonymous account. Sign in with Apple is optional, in Profile (person icon, top right) → Account. Account deletion is at Profile → Account → Delete account and data, and it deletes the account and all of its data from the server. All questions are original practice items; ApexPrep isn't affiliated with the AAMC or LSAC. No demo account is needed.

## App Privacy answers (App Store Connect → App Privacy)

Data collection: **Yes, we collect data.** For each type below, answer: used for **App Functionality** only, **linked** to the user's identity, **not** used for tracking.

| Category | Data type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Identifiers | User ID | The account ID that every backed-up row is stored under |
| Contact Info | Email Address | Only when a student signs in with Apple and shares an email (may be a relay address) |
| User Content | Other User Content | Logged practice-test scores and study settings |
| Usage Data | Product Interaction | Answers, timing, confidence, review history and streaks, backed up to restore progress |

Not collected: name, location, contacts, health data, financial info, browsing history, diagnostics, advertising data.
